How points work

01

Level points
Every level has a point value depending on its position in the list.

02

Total points
Points are calculated automatically using the player's approved progress.

03

Partial progress
Incomplete levels contribute proportionally to the player's total points.

04

Levels beaten
A level counts as beaten when the player's approved progress reaches 100%.
